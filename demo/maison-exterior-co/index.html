Questions

A few quick answers.

What's the difference between permanent and holiday lighting?

Permanent LED lights are fitted along your roofline and stay up all year. You control them from an app on your phone. Holiday lighting goes up for the season only.

How fast will I get a quote?

Within 24 hours. Send us your address and we look at your roofline before we quote, so the price fits your home.

Do you work on businesses too?

Yes. We work on homes and commercial properties across Miami-Dade, and we can set up ongoing maintenance for both.

Do you only do lights?

No. We also do pressure washing, home softwash, paver cleaning and sealing, window cleaning and gutter cleaning, plus landscaping and pool cleaning.

Which areas do you serve?

Palmetto Bay, Cutler Bay, Pinecrest, Homestead, Kendall, Doral, Coral Gables, South Miami, Miami Lakes, Hialeah, Hialeah Gardens, Miami Gardens and Westchester.
