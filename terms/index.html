Terms and refunds

How working with us works.

Plain words, like everything else here. If something isn’t clear, email us before you sign and we’ll explain it.

Last updated November 8, 2026

The short version

  • Every project starts with a written proposal. Price, scope and timing are in it, and nothing starts until you agree.
  • Setup fees and deposits are non-refundable once work has started.
  • Monthly plans renew on the same date each month. Cancel any time with 30 days’ notice.
  • Once you’ve paid in full, the finished work is yours.

Who these terms are between

These terms are between Twin Citys, a creative studio in Miami, Florida, and the business that hires us (“you”). They cover our work and twincitys.net. If you sign a separate agreement or proposal with us, that document wins wherever it says something different from this page.

Proposals and scope

Before any work starts, we send you a proposal that lists what we’ll make, what it costs and roughly when you’ll have it. Work starts once you’ve agreed to it and paid what it asks for up front. Anything outside the proposal is new work, and we’ll quote it before doing it.

Payments

  • Payments run through Stripe. You enter your card or bank details on Stripe’s secure page, and we never see or store them.
  • Setup fees are due before work starts. Monthly plans are charged automatically to the card or bank account on file, on the same date each month.
  • If a payment fails, Stripe will email you and try again. If it stays unpaid, we may pause the work, and any hosting or services we run for you, until it’s settled.

Revisions

Each proposal includes rounds of changes. We want you to love it, so tell us what isn’t working. Changes beyond what’s included, or a change in direction after you’ve approved a stage, may be quoted as extra work.

Monthly plans and cancelling

Monthly plans renew automatically until you cancel. To cancel, email eric@twincitys.net at least 30 days before your next billing date. If your proposal includes a minimum term, the plan runs at least that long. When the plan ends, we’ll hand over what’s yours (see below).

Refunds

  • Setup fees and deposits are non-refundable once work has started, because they pay for time already spent on your project.
  • If we haven’t started yet and you change your mind, tell us and we’ll refund you in full.
  • Monthly charges already billed aren’t refunded. Cancelling stops future charges.
  • If we don’t deliver what the proposal promised, tell us. We’ll fix it, and if we can’t, we’ll work out a fair refund with you.

Approved refunds go back to the original card or bank account. Stripe usually takes 5 to 10 business days to return them.

What you give us

You’re responsible for the logos, photos, text and other material you send us. Please only send material you own or have permission to use. We use it only for your project.

Who owns the work

Once you’ve paid in full, the finished work we made for you is yours to use. That includes the website, designs, photos and videos. Fonts, software, stock media and other tools we license from others stay under their own licenses. Drafts, unused ideas and our own methods stay ours.

We may show the finished work, and your name and logo, on our site and social media as part of our portfolio. If you’d rather we didn’t, just tell us.

Other services

Some of what we set up runs on other companies’ services, such as Google, Instagram, web hosting and payment providers. We can’t control their outages, rule changes or results. We also can’t promise specific numbers, like rankings, followers or sales, because those depend on many things outside our hands.

Limits of our liability

We do careful work, but no one can promise perfection. To the extent the law allows, we aren’t responsible for indirect losses such as lost profits or lost data. Our total responsibility for any claim is limited to what you paid us in the three months before it.

Ending a project

Either of us can end a project with written notice. You pay for the work done up to that point, and we hand over everything you’ve paid for.

Florida law

These terms follow the laws of Florida. If we ever disagree, we’ll try to sort it out by talking first.

Changes

If we change these terms, we’ll update this page and the date at the top. Changes don’t apply to a project you’ve already agreed to unless we both agree.
