Privacy policy

What we collect, and what we do with it.

Plain words, like everything else here. If something isn’t clear, email us and we’ll explain it.

Last updated November 6, 2026

The short version

  • We only collect what you type into our project form, or send us by email.
  • We use it to answer you and to work with you. That’s it.
  • No ad tracking and no cookies. We count visits with a tool that stores nothing about you.
  • We never sell your information or share it for advertising.
  • Ask us to delete it any time, and we will.

Who we are

Twin Citys is a small creative studio in Miami, Florida, run by Eric Martinez, Kevin Butchers and Daniel Navarro. “We” and “us” on this page means us. This policy covers twincitys.net and the emails you send us.

What we collect

  • When you start a project. Your name, your business name, your email or phone number, the services you tick, and anything you write in the message box.
  • When you email us. Your email address and whatever you put in the email.
  • When you sign up for our monthly email. Your email address and first name.
  • When you visit. Like every website, the company that hosts ours receives basic technical details when your browser asks for a page, such as your IP address and browser type. We don’t look at these or use them to identify you.

What we use it for

  • To reply to you and talk through your project.
  • To do the work you hire us for, and to send invoices and receipts.
  • To keep a simple record of who we’ve worked with.

We only add you to our monthly email if you tick the box on our project form and then confirm by email. Every email has an unsubscribe link at the bottom, or just reply and ask us to take you off.

Cookies and tracking

This site sets no cookies and runs no advertising pixels. We count page visits with GoatCounter, a privacy-first counter that keeps no cookies and no record of who you are, only that a page was viewed. The homepage remembers one thing, whether you’ve already seen the opening animation, in your browser’s session storage. It never leaves your device and clears itself when you close the tab.

If we ever add analytics or ad measurement, we’ll update this page first and ask for your permission where the law requires it.

Do Not Track. Because we don’t track visitors across sites, there’s nothing for a “Do Not Track” signal to switch off. We treat every visit the same way.

Who helps us handle it

We use a few trusted services to run the site and our email. They only get what they need to do their part:

  • GitHub Pages hosts the website.
  • Web3Forms delivers project form submissions to our inboxes.
  • Zoho Mail runs our @twincitys.net email.
  • Kit sends our monthly email to people who signed up for it.
  • GoatCounter counts page visits, without cookies or personal data.
  • Stripe processes payments if you pay an invoice online. We never see or store your card number.

We never sell your information, rent it, or share it with anyone for their own advertising. We would only share it beyond these services if the law required us to.

How long we keep it

We keep project messages and email for as long as we’re talking or working together, and afterwards for our records and taxes. If you ask us to delete it, we will, unless the law says we have to keep something (like an invoice).

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to see what we have about you, correct it, or delete it. Email eric@twincitys.net and we’ll reply within a few business days. We won’t treat you any differently for asking.

When we work on your business

If you hire us to run ads, a customer list or a CRM for your business, we handle your customers’ information on your behalf and only to do that work. Your own privacy policy covers your customers, and we’ll help you write or update it if you need one.

Keeping it safe

The site runs over a secure (HTTPS) connection, and only the three of us can see what you send. No system is perfect, so we keep what we hold to a minimum.

Children

Our site is for businesses. We don’t knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. If you think a child has sent us something, tell us and we’ll delete it.

Changes

If we change how we handle information, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.

Questions

Email eric@twincitys.net.
