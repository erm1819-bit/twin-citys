Privacy PolicyPolítica de privacidad

Effective November 19, 2026Vigente desde el 19 de noviembre de 2026

This policy explains what information the Lucerne Bakery website collects and how it is used. "We" means Lucerne Bakery, 7415 Coral Way, Miami, FL 33155 · (305) 986-1507.

What this website collects

You don't need an account to use this site, and it doesn't run advertising or analytics trackers. It stores one small preference in your own browser: the language you picked. It stays on your device and isn't sent to us.

Like any website, the service that hosts this site receives standard technical details (such as your IP address and browser type) needed to deliver the page.

Ordering, rewards and maps

Online orders, delivery, payment and Store Rewards are handled on our ordering page, which is run by DoorDash. When you order or join rewards there, DoorDash collects and processes your information under the DoorDash Privacy Policy, and shares order details with us so we can make your order.

The map image on this site comes from OpenStreetMap and is stored with the site. Directions open in Google Maps or Apple Maps, and reviews are posted on Google. Those services have their own privacy policies.

Catering requests, calls and texts

If you send a catering request or call us, we use your name, contact details and event details only to answer you and prepare your order.

What we don't do

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't share it with third parties for their own marketing.

Children

This site isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information.

Your choices

You can clear this site's saved preferences in your browser settings at any time. To ask about information we hold from a catering request or call, contact us using the details below. For your DoorDash account or rewards data, use DoorDash's privacy tools.

Changes and contact

If we change this policy, we'll update it here with a new effective date. Questions: Lucerne Bakery, 7415 Coral Way, Miami, FL 33155 · (305) 986-1507.
